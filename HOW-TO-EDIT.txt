KRUNAL & MISHIKA – EMERALD GARDEN INVITATION
============================================
Open details.js in any text editor – everything is in that one file.

Most common edits:
  rsvpEmail   -> the couple's real email (RSVP replies open the guest's email app addressed to this)
  venueName   -> the venue's real name (currently "The Wedding Venue")
  venueAddress-> optional address line ("" hides it)
  mapLink     -> Google Maps share link for the "Find your way" button
  rsvpBy      -> RSVP deadline
  music       -> put your song in the music folder as song.mp3 (or change the name here)
  weddingDateTime -> the countdown counts to this (now: Phere, 18 Jan 2027, 12:39 PM IST)

Events: each line in "events" is one ceremony. { header: "The Marriage" } starts the
Marriage group – every event after it appears inside that group.

Photos live in the images folder. Keep names lowercase and the same as in details.js.
Scratch cards show on every visit (built in).
